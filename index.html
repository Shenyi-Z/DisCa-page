DisCa: Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers with Distillation-Compatible Learnable Feature Caching

Chang Zou, Changlin Li, Yang Li, Patrol Li, Jianbing Wu, Xiao He, Songtao Liu, Zhao Zhong, Kailin Huang, Linfeng Zhang†
EPIC Lab at SAI, SJTU | Tencent Hunyuan
Paper arXiv Code Models

Abstract

While diffusion models have achieved great success in video generation, this progress is accompanied by a rapidly escalating computational burden. Among existing acceleration methods, feature caching is popular due to its training-free property and considerable speedup, but it inevitably faces semantic and detail degradation under stronger compression. Training-aware step-distillation is also widely used, yet few-step video generation can still suffer drastic quality loss, especially when combined with training-free caching.

This paper introduces a distillation-compatible learnable feature caching mechanism. We employ a lightweight learnable neural predictor instead of traditional training-free heuristics, enabling a more accurate capture of high-dimensional feature evolution. We also explore highly compressed distillation on large-scale video models and propose Restricted MeanFlow for more stable, lossless distillation.
